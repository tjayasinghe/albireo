# Research behind the adoption plan (2026-09-03)

Seven reports written on 2026-09-03 by research agents working in parallel, each from a
self-contained brief, and cited by letter in [`../../adoption.md`](../../adoption.md). They
are evidence, not documentation: the register is the agents' own, the length is what the
questions needed, and each report marks which of its statements were verified against a
primary source on that date and which are inference. Nothing in them was edited afterwards.

| letter | file | question |
|---|---|---|
| A | `A_field_needs.md` | What binary-star spectroscopists did and asked for in print, 2023 to 2026; the competing codes |
| B | `B_data_access.md` | Which public archives hold epoch spectra of binaries, how they are read, and what Gaia DR4 delivers |
| C | `C_physics_extensions.md` | The literature and the equations for the candidate model extensions, classified by what stays linear in the spectra |
| D | `D_codebase_audit.md` | A read-only audit of the repository at `6c41549`: light fractions, the solver, the façade's refusals, release readiness, throughput records |
| E | `E_aiphe_k_bias.md` | The 1% semi-amplitude offset on AI Phoenicis: epochs, ephemeris, literature values, ranked causes |
| F | `F_fresh_install.md` | A fresh install and first run by a new user, with timings and a ranked friction list |
| G | `G_ecosystem.md` | PyPI, conda-forge, JOSS, pyOpenSci, specutils, adoption case studies, venues and dates |

Known limits, stated by the reports themselves: ADS abstract pages and A&A full texts refuse
automated fetches, so equations were read from arXiv; citation counts are OpenAlex or Semantic
Scholar, not ADS; two agents exhausted their web-search budgets before finishing (A on the
OWN survey and XShootU papers, E on Andersen 1988 and the Hełminiak and Sybilski primary
values). Absolute file paths in the reports refer to the session's scratch directory and are
not meaningful here.
